1. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in connection with the mobile application Tank Force (Android package ID: com.tankforce.game), developed and offered by The Data Sequence ("we", "us", or "our").

We are committed to transparency regarding privacy practices when you download, install, or play Tank Force on your mobile device.

2. Introduction

Tank Force is a mobile tank combat game designed for Android devices and distributed through the Google Play Store.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when someone uses the App. It covers analytics and potential advertising technologies used by or integrated into the App.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using Tank Force. By downloading, accessing, or playing Tank Force, you acknowledge the privacy practices described in this document. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not install or use the App.

3. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with the App, your device settings, and integrated third-party services, certain technical, usage, and diagnostic information may be processed automatically.

Potential categories of information that may be processed include:

  • Device Information: Device model, manufacturer, screen resolution, hardware properties, and system preferences.
  • Operating System Information: OS type and OS version.
  • App Information: App version, build number, package identifier, and installation source.
  • Language & Region: System language preferences and country or regional settings.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse or approximate location derived from IP address or network information where provided by third-party services.
  • IP Address & Network Info: Internet Protocol address and general connection type.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Mobile advertising IDs such as the Google Advertising ID, subject to device configuration and consent.
  • App Usage & Gameplay Interactions: Gameplay events, sessions, levels attempted or completed, and general feature interactions.
  • Diagnostic Information: Crash reports, system performance metrics, and error logs where applicable.

Important Distinction: Not all categories of information listed above are stored or personally processed directly by the developer. Some information may be processed automatically by integrated third-party SDKs and service providers.

4. Information Collected Automatically

When you launch and play Tank Force, integrated software development kits (SDKs) and system services may process technical data automatically to deliver gameplay features and assess performance.

This automated processing may occur in the background when the application runs on your Android device and does not necessarily require explicit manual input from you during gameplay.

5. Device and Technical Information

Technical and device information may be processed for the following operational purposes:

  • To ensure proper operation and core functionality of the game.
  • To maintain device and software compatibility across Android hardware.
  • To troubleshoot technical glitches, application crashes, and performance issues.
  • To improve app optimization and user experience.
  • To protect the game and service infrastructure against abuse, fraud, or security threats.

We do not collect or store hardware serial numbers or persistent un-resettable hardware identifiers unless explicitly disclosed or required for technical service operations.

6. Gameplay and Usage Information

The App may process gameplay metrics and usage interactions for analytics and game design improvement purposes.

The App may record gameplay events such as:

  • Game session initiation, duration, and app launch frequency.
  • Level starts, completions, failures, and restarts.
  • Use of in-game features and gameplay mechanics.
  • Gameplay progression, achievements, and milestone events.
  • General navigation within app menus and user interface screens.

These events help us understand gameplay performance, level difficulty, player engagement, and areas where improvements may be needed.

7. Analytics Information

Analytics information refers to statistical data collected to analyze how Tank Force is downloaded, launched, and played.

Analytics data may be evaluated in an aggregated or pseudonymized form to assess general usage trends rather than identifying individual users.

8. Firebase Analytics

Tank Force may use Google Firebase Analytics to understand app usage, measure player engagement, and improve game stability and design.

Where Firebase Analytics is integrated, information processed may include:

  • App interactions and custom gameplay events.
  • Session counts, durations, and retention metrics.
  • Device brand, model, operating system version, and system language.
  • Approximate location derived from IP address.
  • App version and build information.
  • Diagnostic and error data related to app sessions.

The developer may receive aggregate reporting and dashboard insights to understand overall app performance and usage.

For additional information, please consult Firebase's privacy documentation.

9. Advertising

Tank Force may display advertisements to support ongoing development and help offer the game free of charge.

The App may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob and Unity Ads or Unity LevelPlay, where integrated.

Advertising providers may process information to:

  • Deliver and display advertisements within the game.
  • Measure ad performance, impressions, clicks, and conversion rates.
  • Prevent advertising fraud and invalid traffic.
  • Limit repeated advertisements.
  • Personalize advertisements where permitted by law and user settings.

Depending on applicable laws, user choices, device settings, consent status, and service configuration, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

10. Google AdMob

The App may use Google AdMob to serve banner, interstitial, rewarded, or other supported advertisements.

Where AdMob is integrated, technical information such as mobile advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, and ad interaction data may be processed for serving and measuring advertisements.

For additional information, please consult Google's applicable privacy and advertising documentation.

11. Unity Ads / Unity LevelPlay

The App may use Unity Ads and/or Unity LevelPlay for displaying advertisements and advertising mediation where these services are integrated.

Unity services may process information such as device model, operating system, mobile advertising identifiers, IP address, network information, ad interactions, and diagnostic metrics.

If Unity advertising services are not active in a particular build, Unity advertising-related processing will not occur through that integration in that build.

12. Advertising Identifiers

Mobile advertising identifiers, such as the Google Advertising ID on Android devices, are user-resettable identifiers provided by the operating system.

Advertising services may use these identifiers to:

  • Recognize an advertising-enabled device.
  • Serve interest-based advertising where permitted.
  • Measure advertising campaign effectiveness.
  • Limit repeated advertisements and detect fraudulent traffic.

You may control, reset, delete, or limit advertising identifiers through your Android device settings, subject to your device and Android version.
